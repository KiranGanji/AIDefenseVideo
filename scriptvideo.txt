AI Defense - Built to detect what traditional systems miss 

The threat is no longer isolated. It is structural. Providers and revenue cycle partners are using AI to reverse-engineer payer policies and optimize coding at scale — driving higher medical cost and lower affordability. 

AI Defense is our response: a modular capability suite built across Policy and Benefit , Claims, Network, and Clinical Intelligences, along with state of art Agentic Orchestration — all connected through a modular architecture designed for secure, auditable execution. 

We've built 4 Signal Agents. Provider Coding Intensity Signal Agents such as Impact Radar, Adversarial Claim Simulator and Third Party Graph identify provider reimbursement-maximizing tactics before they scale. Retro AI Editing is capturing affordability optimization opportunities identifying smaller aberrant claims at a scale and speed traditional post pay analytics cannot. 

Across the portfolio, we have already identified over 30 million dollars in combined exposure opportunity — and that number is growing as each module matures. 

Impact Radar benchmarks providers against historical and true peer cohorts to detect abnormal coding and utilization shifts. 
A recent signal from this solution identified nine billing entities whose ER-to-inpatient conversion rates were three-and-a-half to six times higher than comparable peers. Most inpatient stays were only one to three days, yet one provider had ninety-four percent of allowed spend in inpatient services. Across the sixty-eight thousand claims identified there is roughly two-point-one million dollars in exposure that is currently under review. 

Adversarial Claim Simulator thinks like a revenue optimizer — simulating reimbursement-maximizing tactics before they scale.It uncovered five laboratory providers using Modifier 59 and X-series modifiers at rates between thirty-five and eighty-nine percent — compared to peer medians under five percent. Estimated exposure for this signal is one-point-eight million dollars across forty-three thousand claim lines that is currently under review. 

Third Party Graph maps the ecosystem of Revenue Cycle Management firms and their provider clients — consolidating claims analytics, provider data, RCM external data, and FWA proceedings into a unified graph-based framework. It uncovers hidden relationships and patterns across providers, CPT codes, and vendor connections — building comprehensive provider risk profiles and predicting emerging trends to enable proactive fraud detection and strategic intervention. It identified a subset of providers who ordered special stains alongside routine surgical pathology exams at rates materially higher than peer benchmarks. This pattern may reflect reflex or protocol‐driven testing and warrants further validation 

Retro AI Editing turns reimbursement policy into machine-executable logic and exposes where payments diverge from policy intent. It surfaced an Add-On Codes Policy leakage on Professional claims. Per policy, add-on codes are reimbursable only when billed with an eligible primary procedure, by the same provider, on the same date of service. The module identified 344 claim lines where add-on code 99292 was paid even though the primary code 99291 was denied or unpaid — driving roughly two million dollars in exposure. These claims passed automation revealing system gaps - exactly the kind of drift AI-assisted billing optimization exploits, and exactly what this module catches. 

What makes AI Defense different is it's not one model or one rule set — rather a coordinated defense system built to detect earlier, reason deeper, and act faster than the market evolves.
